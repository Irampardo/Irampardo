## 🧠 About Me

> *"Pragmatism over hype — I apply AI and data science to find novel biomedical solutions."*

I'm an **Bioinformatics & Data Scientist** at **Pompeu Fabra University**, performing complex biomedical data projects to find novel strategies to combat diseases . My background spans Biology, Bioinformatics, Data Science, Deep Learning, MLOps, and low-code automation.

- 🤖 Currently perfoming complex workflows in single-cell datasets
- 🎓 MSc in AI & Data Science - Leveraging clinical and genomics data to find novel patterns of disease
